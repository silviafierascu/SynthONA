## How the protocol was built

The underlying data ontology was constructed through four rounds of iterative
collaborative design:

1. **Domain scaffolding** — establishing the core parameters and initial taxonomy
2. **Strategic intent layer** — weaving in the team's project goals
3. **Human-centred design layer** — incorporating stakeholder personas and their
   adoption journeys
4. **Technical specification layer** — hardening the schema with scientific precision
